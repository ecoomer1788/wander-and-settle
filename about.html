About Wander & Settle

Wander & Settle is a student website project created to make researching a move abroad feel more approachable.

Our purpose

Explore destinations, organize moving preparations, and find reliable resources in one welcoming place. This website is educational and does not provide legal, immigration, or financial advice.

Privacy and accessibility

Our interactive quiz runs locally in your browser. It does not ask for identifying information or submit your answers to a server. The moving checklist is temporary and resets when you reload the page. This site does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies; third-party websites have their own privacy practices.

We aim to provide descriptive image alternatives, keyboard-friendly navigation, readable text, and responsive layouts. If something is difficult to use, it should be improved.

Sources and image credits

Destination information should be checked against official sources, including Make it in Germany, Netherlands IND, and IRCC Canada. Destination illustrations are original SVG artwork included with this student project.
