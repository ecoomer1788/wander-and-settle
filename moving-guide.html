Moving guide

Moving abroad involves many moving pieces. Start with the basics and research the requirements that apply to your situation.

Preparation steps

  1. Choose a destination and research current immigration requirements.
  2. Estimate moving costs and ongoing living expenses.
  3. Research employment options and any required qualifications.
  4. Compare neighborhoods, housing, and transportation.
  5. Plan for language learning and cultural adjustment.
  6. If moving with family, research schools, childcare, and healthcare.
  7. Organize documents and create a realistic timeline.

Budgeting & housing

Think about deposits, travel, shipping, and monthly expenses.
